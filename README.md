# Global YouTube Performance Analytics

## Overview

YouTube is one of the largest digital content platforms in the world, generating massive amounts of data related to subscribers, video views, uploads, audience engagement, and creator earnings. Analyzing this data can provide valuable insights into content performance, creator growth, audience behavior, and monetization trends.

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Global YouTube Statistics dataset using Python-based Data Science libraries. The analysis aims to uncover meaningful trends, patterns, correlations, and business insights related to YouTube channel performance across different countries and content categories.

The project includes data preprocessing, statistical analysis, trend analysis, correlation analysis, and advanced data visualizations to better understand the dynamics of digital content creation and platform growth.

---

# Objectives

* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing on YouTube statistics data
* Analyze subscriber growth, video views, uploads, and earnings
* Explore category-wise and country-wise channel performance
* Identify trends, patterns, and correlations between key metrics
* Visualize audience behavior and creator growth using statistical plots
* Generate meaningful business insights through Exploratory Data Analysis

# Key Insights

* Entertainment and Music channels were among the most dominant categories on YouTube.
* Strong positive correlations were observed between subscribers, video views, and yearly earnings.
* The United States contributed significantly to total subscribers and channel earnings.
* Channels with higher subscriber counts generally achieved higher revenue generation.
* Upload frequency alone did not guarantee higher subscriber growth.
* Most YouTube channels experienced rapid growth after 2010.
* Significant outliers were observed in subscribers, earnings, and video views.
